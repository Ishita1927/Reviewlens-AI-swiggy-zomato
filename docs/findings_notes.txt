FINDINGS NOTES (sample of 600 reviews, 29 Oct - 10 Nov 2026)

Q1. Is refund the top theme?
No. By mentions: support 149, delivery_delay 119, other 97, non_delivery 74, refund 73.
By severity points (mentions x avg severity): support 424, delivery_delay 299,
other 230, non_delivery 216, refund 208.
Refund is only 5th by volume, but its average severity (2.85) is among the highest.
34 of 73 refund reviews also mention support. So the real pain looks like:
"something fails, then the resolution (refund/support) fails too."

Support check (manual check of 30 tagged reviews): 27 of 30 were truly support complaints.

Limits I must mention:
- Sample covers only 12 days, so I cannot claim a monthly trend yet.
- Severity labels are rough: model vs my labels match only 36.7% exactly
  (within 1 point 100%). The model tends to give 1 where I gave 0 (praise).
- Honest accuracy on the unseen test set is 74%, not 85% (85% was tuned on the gold set).
- Support check only measures precision of the support tag, not recall.
